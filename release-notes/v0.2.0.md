### Packing a dump

- **Titles and icons from the game's control data:** if your dump folder includes the game's `control.nacp` and icon files, the title, publisher, display version, icon and translated titles fill in by themselves when you choose the folder. The app looks in the newest update first, so the display version is the current one.
- **Load from control.nacp:** a new button in the Metadata section reads control data you saved somewhere else and fills in the same fields.
- **Your own entries stay:** anything you typed or chose yourself isn't replaced, and switching to another dump only clears what was filled in for you.

### Installing

- **License included:** the app now comes with its own MIT license next to the other bundled licenses.
- **Upgrading:** run the new installer and it replaces the version you have, in the same place.
